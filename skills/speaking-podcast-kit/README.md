# Speaking & Podcast Pitch Kit

Your AI helps you get booked on podcasts and stages, and turn every appearance into leads, clients, and book sales. It builds your signature message and talk, titles and descriptions, bios, a one-sheet, a target list of shows and events, pitch emails with follow-ups, and the call to action and follow-up system that turns appearances into business.

## Install

Paste this into Grok Bot (recommended), Claude, Codex, or Grok Build:

> Install the speaking-podcast-kit skill from https://github.com/selfpubcom/public-skills/tree/main/skills/speaking-podcast-kit, then run it.

## What you'll need

- About 45-60 minutes for the full kit, or pick just the parts you need
- What you want from appearances (clients, book sales, email subscribers, credibility, or speaking fees)
- Your offer and ideal client
- Optional: your book, any past talks or episodes, and a free resource you can offer listeners

## The 6 parts

1. Your signature message and talk
2. Titles, hooks, and descriptions
3. Your speaker kit (bios, one-sheet, guest sheet, demo video plan)
4. Your target list of podcasts and stages
5. Pitch emails and follow-ups
6. Turning appearances into clients

## FAQ

**I've never spoken or been on a podcast. Is this for me?**
Yes. It starts you with easy, local, and smaller opportunities so you get practice, footage, and testimonials, then helps you climb from there.

**Will it send emails for me?**
No. It drafts every pitch and follow-up in your voice. You review and send them yourself.

**Can it find podcasts and events for me?**
If your AI can browse the web, it researches and qualifies targets for you and links to where to find each contact. It never guesses email addresses.

**Does it work better with the Brand Builder?**
Yes. If you've installed your brand skill, it already knows your story, bios, offers, and voice, so it asks fewer questions and everything sounds like you.

**Do I have to do all 6 parts?**
No. Tell your AI what you need, like "just write my podcast pitches," and it goes straight there.
