# SOP & Systems Builder

Your AI helps you get out of the day-to-day by turning what's in your head into a company playbook. It finds what to stop doing and what to hand off first, builds a prioritized list of processes to document, sets up a simple playbook, and writes step-by-step SOPs a new person could follow without asking you a single question. Then it helps you hand each one off.

## Install

Paste this into Grok Bot (recommended), Claude, Codex, or Grok Build:

> Install the sop-builder skill from https://github.com/selfpubcom/public-skills/tree/main/skills/sop-builder, then run it.

## What you'll need

- About 45-60 minutes for your first session (time audit, backlog, and your first SOPs)
- A rough idea of how you spend a typical week
- Optional: a screen recording of you doing a task while talking out loud, or any old notes or checklists you already have

## The 6 parts

1. Your time audit and stop-doing list
2. Your playbook backlog (what to systematize first)
3. Your playbook setup
4. Capture and write your SOPs
5. Your references and templates
6. Hand it off and keep it alive

## FAQ

**My business is too unique to systematize. Will this work for me?**
Almost certainly. Nearly every task can be broken into repeatable steps, and your judgment calls can be written as simple rules and examples. The skill asks the questions that pull that out of your head.

**What's the easiest way to document a process?**
Record your screen while you do the task for real, and talk out loud about what you're doing and why. Give your AI the recording or transcript, and it writes the SOP for you.

**I don't have anyone to delegate to yet. Should I still do this?**
Yes. Writing SOPs first makes your first hire faster to train, and the time audit shows you exactly what that first hire should take off your plate.

**Where does my playbook live?**
Wherever your team already works: Google Drive, Notion, or plain documents. If your AI is connected to one of those, it can create the folder and files for you.

**Does it work better with the Brand Builder?**
Yes. If you've installed your brand skill, it already knows your business, offers, and customers, so it asks fewer questions.
