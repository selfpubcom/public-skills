# Website Audit

Your AI visits your website the way a customer, Google, and AI search tools see it. It reviews your pages like an experienced marketer and copywriter would, catching the mistakes most owners miss, like a signup form stuck at the bottom of the page or a sales video buried halfway down. It scores 11 areas, from page flow and calls to action to SEO and AI search readiness, then gives you the 10 fixes that matter most, with exactly what to change.

## Install

Paste this into Grok Bot (recommended), Claude, Codex, or Grok Build:

> Install the website-audit skill from https://github.com/selfpubcom/public-skills/tree/main/skills/website-audit, then run it.

## What you'll need

- About 15-20 minutes
- Your website link
- The #1 thing you want visitors to do (book a call, buy, join your list)

## What it checks

1. Page flow and layout (where your video, forms, buttons, proof, and price sit)
2. First impression (the 5-second test)
3. Offers and calls to action
4. Trust and proof
5. Copy and messaging (checked against proven copywriting principles)
6. Lead capture and forms
7. Navigation and structure
8. On-page SEO
9. AI search readiness
10. Accessibility basics
11. Speed and mobile

## FAQ

**Does it need access to my Google Analytics or Search Console?**
No. It only looks at what anyone can see by visiting your site. It doesn't see your traffic, conversions, or rankings.

**Will it change anything on my website?**
No. It only makes recommendations. You decide what to change.

**Does it work better with the Brand Builder?**
Yes. If you've installed your brand skill, the audit already knows your customer, offers, and voice. It asks fewer questions and writes any suggested rewrites in your voice.

**Why does my report say "Not checked" for speed?**
Some AI tools can't open a real web browser. In those, the audit skips the speed test and mobile view, and tells you so. If your AI can browse the web, those checks run too.

**How often should I run it?**
After any big change to your site, and every few months otherwise.
